From product link to parcel

Verify the original seller link

When comparing UIDBuy product links from Weidian, Taobao or 1688, record the marketplace item ID, seller name and last-checked date. Reopen the original listing, select the exact colour and size, then confirm stock, order quantity and the price for that variant.

Keep the seller’s size chart beside your notes. Compare sneaker insole length or a hoodie’s chest width and sleeve length with something you already wear. Record measurements in centimetres and convert to inches if needed.

Make a QC photo checklist

Ask for warehouse QC photos tied to your order. For shoes, compare the outsole, size label and both side profiles. For a jacket or jersey, request a flat chest measurement and a close-up of stitching or fasteners.

For headwear, check the inner circumference and adjustment range. For accessories, confirm the chain length, clasp condition and material description. Save the photos with the corresponding order line so you can compare them with the seller listing.

Compare international shipping

Build a parcel packing list with item quantities and packaging preferences. Ask for packed dimensions and actual weight, then compare the route’s chargeable weight, tracking and coverage for your destination.

For deliveries to the US, UK or EU, check which charges the quote includes and which may be collected on arrival. Compare total delivered cost in your own currency using the same exchange rate for each option.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UIDBuy spreadsheet?

A UIDBuy spreadsheet is a list of product leads that can help you compare original seller links, sizes and colours. Reopen each listing to check the exact option before placing an order.

Where do the category links take me?

They open category pages on HipoBuyFind. This independent guide explains a shopping workflow for readers searching for UIDBuy spreadsheet information.

Which QC photos should I request?

Ask for photos of the exact item you ordered. For shoes, request both sides, the outsole and the size label. For garments, request flat measurements. For jewellery, request a clasp close-up.

How do I estimate the total cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service and payment fees, and international shipping. Check the destination’s rules for any taxes, duties or handling charges.

Can QC photos establish authenticity?

QC photos can document visible details and condition. For an authenticity claim, seek verifiable purchase records or use an authorised retailer.
